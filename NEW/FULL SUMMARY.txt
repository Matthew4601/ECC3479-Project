  OVERALL CONCLUSION:
    The 2022 inflation shock produced a statistically significant and robust structural
    shift in energy→core pass-through for Canada and UK (significant at 1% across all
    robustness tests). The USA shift is significant at 1% with the correct 2022-01
    break date but fragile under some specifications, suggesting the US transmission
    mechanism was better anchored. The 2022 episode was not fundamentally new:
    pass-through amplification (γ₂₀₂₂=+0.024) was the weakest of the three major
    inflation episodes, well below 1973 (+0.088) and 1979 (+0.054). Unit root tests
    suggest the long-run levels relationship is more reliable than short-run dynamics.
    Cross-sectional dependence (Pesaran CD p=0.000) confirms a strong common global
    shock component, supporting country-level over pooled panel inference.
