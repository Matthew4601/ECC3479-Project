ROBUSTNESS TEST 10: CROSS-SECTIONAL DEPENDENCE — PESARAN CD TEST
================================================================================
  Tests whether panel residuals are correlated across countries (i.e. common shocks).
  Pesaran CD statistic: CD = sqrt(2T/(N(N-1))) * Σᵢ<ⱼ ρ̂ᵢⱼ
  H0: cross-sectional independence. Large |CD| → reject H0.
  With N=3 countries, the CD test is based on 3 pairwise correlations.

------------------------------------------------------------

------------------------------------------------------------
    Pair                        ρ  Interpretation
    ---------------------------------------------
    Canada vs UK           0.7451  strong cross-sectional correlation
    Canada vs USA          0.7777  strong cross-sectional correlation
    UK vs USA              0.7931  strong cross-sectional correlation

    Pesaran CD statistic: 29.3859   p=0.0000  *** sig. at 1% and 5%

  RE-ESTIMATION WITH TIME-CLUSTERED SEs (robust to cross-sectional dependence)

    β (Energy CPI, two-way FE):  -0.0013   p=0.3447      NOT significant
    (Baseline one-way FE:         -0.0059     p=0.0076)

  VERDICT: If CD is significant, cross-sectional dependence is present — common shocks
           affect all three countries simultaneously. Two-way FE absorbs time-common
           shocks. If β remains significant under two-way FE, the result is robust.
